## 🧠 AI/ML Frontier Papers

**[CritICL: Inference-Time Weak-to-Strong Generalization from Small Language Model Failure Modes](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.27455)**
🔬 Breakthrough: Discovered that within the same model family, small model failure modes replicate structurally in larger models—leveraging this pattern, using the small model's erroneous outputs as "reverse prompts" guides the large model to avoid similar errors, improving accuracy by 8.3% on MATH-500 with inference costs at only 1/5 of self-consistency methods.
⚙️ Engineering Impact: No longer requires multiple sampling or external verifiers at inference time; a single forward pass yields weak-to-strong generalization gains. For production environments, this means accuracy improvements without added latency, suitable as a plug-and-play enhancement layer for existing CoT pipelines.

**[TacForcing: Streaming Action Generation with Execution-Time Tactile Feedback](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.25798)**
🔬 Breakthrough: Overturns the assumption that "action blocks must be predicted in one shot from pre-execution observations" by injecting tactile feedback in a streaming manner into the action generation process, improving success rates on contact-rich manipulation tasks from 61% to 84%, without requiring additional high-frequency controllers.
⚙️ Engineering Impact: For robot manipulation pipelines, this means eliminating the separate tactile feedback control layer and integrating multimodal perception directly into the VLA model's streaming inference, reducing architectural complexity and training costs.

**[EditaLive! Unified Character Video Editing for Live Streaming](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.27123)**
🔬 Breakthrough: Based on the Wan-Animate image animation model, achieves real-time character video editing in live streaming scenarios, with single-frame inference latency <40ms, supporting expression replacement and clothing changes, solving the bottleneck of existing video editing methods where "multi-step offline inference cannot support real-time interaction."
⚙️ Engineering Impact: For the live streaming and virtual human sectors, character editing moves from "post-production for recorded content" to "real-time interaction," enabling direct integration into streaming toolchains like OBS, replacing current green screen + motion capture setups.
