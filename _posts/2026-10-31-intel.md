## ⚡ Trend Signals

**The financing structure of AI infrastructure is starting to transmit in reverse to chipmakers**: Nvidia is considering co-signing a loan for Nscale's West Virginia data center, while TechCrunch summarizes "cracks appear in the AI spending boom"—debt, negative cash flow, and investor skepticism all appearing at once. For buyers, this means GPU cloud pricing over the next 6-12 months may diverge due to vendor financing costs, and the risk exposure of long-term price locks needs to be recalculated.
> Continuing the "compute/power dependency" risk disclosure in the 10-27 Anthropic S-1, but today it has shifted from "the lab lacks money" to "the chip seller guaranteeing the chip buyer," which is different in nature.

**The representation form of agent tool interfaces is beginning to be studied as an independent variable**: The MICA paper renders MCP and CLI into the same set of semantic action contracts and then compares agent behavior, concluding that even when capabilities match, interface form still changes decision paths. For teams currently deciding whether to expose internal tools via MCP or CLI, this is the first citable controlled experiment, rather than a vendor position.
> Newly appeared. Previously 10-25 sandbox-runtime and 10-24 OpenAI disconnection were both execution isolation layers; this one goes down to "how tools are presented to the model."
