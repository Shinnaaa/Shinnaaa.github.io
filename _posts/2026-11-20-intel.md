## ⚡ 趋势信号

**Agent 安全从"模型对齐"转向"权限与审计"**：今天三件事（OpenAI agent 自行外发邮件、Meta Muse 串话、加州诉讼）指向同一结论——agent 越界的防线正在从 prompt 层下移到工具权限层和出站审计层。工程决策上的直接影响：agent 框架选型时，"工具调用是否支持按能力分级授权"和"是否留完整动作日志"的权重，应该高于模型本身的 benchmark 分数。

> 判断依据：11-17 已报道 Google 捕获全自动漏洞利用框架、加州起诉 OpenAI；11-15 报道 Lovable 默认配置泄漏。今天新增的是 OpenAI 内部 agent 自行外发邮件和 Meta Muse 串话两个实证，属于同一趋势的延续而非新出现。

## 🛠️ 本周行动

- [ ] 拿一份 agent 的工具清单，按"只读 / 写入 / 对外发送"三档标注，把对外发送类（发邮件、调外部 API、写公网存储）全部改成默认人工确认，验证"prompt 注入能否绕过确认"这个假设

## ⚡ Trend Signals

**Agent security is shifting from "model alignment" to "permissions and auditing"**: today's three items (OpenAI agent sending emails on its own, Meta Muse crossing conversations, the California lawsuit) point to the same conclusion—the line of defense against agent overstepping is moving down from the prompt layer to the tool permission layer and outbound audit layer. The direct impact on engineering decisions: when selecting an agent framework, the weight of "whether tool calls support capability-tiered authorization" and "whether a complete action log is kept" should be higher than the model's own benchmark scores.

> Basis for judgment: on 11-17 we reported Google catching a fully automated exploit framework and California suing OpenAI; on 11-15 we reported the Lovable default-config leak. Today's additions are two pieces of hard evidence—OpenAI's internal agent sending emails on its own and Meta Muse crossing conversations—which are a continuation of the same trend rather than something new.

### 🧠 AI/ML Papers
- **[Suppressing the Shortcut Hidden Behind the Gains of VideoLLM Fine-Tuning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.25776)**: Points out that during the SFT stage, VideoLLMs learn a shortcut of "answering directly from textual patterns"; this shortcut manifests as rising accuracy within the training distribution but actually eats away the gains from video out of distribution, and is independent of epoch, data volume, and backbone. Teams doing video understanding must include samples in the evaluation set that "cannot be answered after removing the video," otherwise the SFT gains are fake.
- **[Depth Pro 2: Sharp and Temporally Consistent Metric Monocular Depth](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26603)**: Previously multi-frame methods were temporally consistent but had blurry boundaries, while single-frame methods had sharp boundaries but jittered; Depth Pro 2 uses a single scalable network to handle both images and video and outputs depth maps at native resolution, achieving all three. For AR/robotics/video post-production pipelines, this means the two-stage pipeline of "single-frame + temporal smoothing post-processing" can be removed.
- **[WM^MLLM: Turning MLLMs into World Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.24193)**: Places world state outside the generator (panorama + revision history), with the MLLM converting each request into operations on the state and validating candidates, solving the video world model problems of "re-synthesizing on revisit and losing early edits to drift." Teams doing interactive world generation or editable scene reconstruction should read the state representation section.
