## ⚡ 趋势信号

**推理可复现性开始被当成硬需求**：tritify 用全整数算术跑通 decoder-only LLM、MoE 和 SAM 2.1 的前向，浮点只留在离线量化和验证参考里，做到跨硬件逐 bit 一致。这对需要审计、合规或分布式验证的推理部署是新的架构选项——代价是放弃 GPU 浮点吞吐，本周值得测的是它在你们模型上的实际延迟倍数。
> 延续 11-22 Lean 黎曼猜想验证那条线：形式化验证和可复现推理在同一个方向上——让计算结果可被第三方独立复核，而不是只能信任执行环境。

**OpenAI 首次公开 GPT 全系内部 perplexity 分数，称能力在精确阈值处涌现，并推动行业共享限制**  
把"能力涌现阈值"从论文话题变成可对齐的工程指标，如果各家跟进，模型选型的评测集逻辑会从 benchmark 分数转向 perplexity 阈值定位。对做模型路由的团队，这是未来半年值得盯的接口变化。
> 新出现，与 11-18 Astra"自我改进 High 阈值"标签同源——阈值化描述能力正在成为实验室对外沟通的标准语言。

## ⚡ Trend Signals

**Inference reproducibility is starting to be treated as a hard requirement**: tritify runs the forward pass of decoder-only LLMs, MoE, and SAM 2.1 entirely in integer arithmetic, leaving floating point only for offline quantization and verification references, achieving bit-exact consistency across hardware. For inference deployments requiring auditing, compliance, or distributed verification, this is a new architectural option—at the cost of giving up GPU floating-point throughput. What's worth testing this week is the actual latency multiplier on your models.
> Continuing the 11-22 Lean Riemann hypothesis verification thread: formal verification and reproducible inference point in the same direction—making computation results independently verifiable by third parties, rather than having to trust the execution environment.

**OpenAI publicly discloses internal perplexity scores across the entire GPT line for the first time, claims capabilities emerge at precise thresholds, and pushes for industry-wide sharing of limits**  
Turning "capability emergence thresholds" from a paper topic into an alignable engineering metric—if others follow suit, the evaluation-set logic for model selection will shift from benchmark scores to perplexity threshold localization. For teams doing model routing, this is an interface change worth watching over the next six months.
> Newly emerged, same origin as the 11-18 Astra "self-improvement High threshold" tag—threshold-based descriptions of capabilities are becoming the standard language labs use for external communication.
