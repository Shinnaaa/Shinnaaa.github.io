## 📌 今日要闻

**1. 信用卡侧录脚本改用 WebRTC 绕过 CSP 与第三方脚本白名单，黑色星期五当天在多个购物站被抓到**  
传统 Magecart 靠注入 `<script src>` 外传数据，CSP 和 SRI 能拦；WebRTC 的 DataChannel 不受 connect-src 约束，数据直接从浏览器 P2P 出去，服务端日志里什么都没有。正在跑电商结账页的团队，本周该做的是在 CSP 之外加一层运行时检测（监听 `RTCPeerConnection` 构造），而不是继续加 report-uri。 · [infohawk](https://www.infohawk.com/news/2026-11-27-black-friday-webrtc-skimmer) / [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50660112)

**2. Google DeepCrossAttention：残差连接改成跨层注意力，同等质量下训练快 3 倍**  
把残差流的加法换成对历史层的注意力聚合，等于让每层自己决定从哪几层取信息，而不是固定取上一层。3x 是训练吞吐口径，不是推理——对正在排预训练预算的团队，这意味着同等 GPU 小时能多跑 3 倍 token，但推理侧收益未披露，别拿它当推理优化。 · [Google Research](https://research.google/blog/supercharging-transformer-residual-connections/) / [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50658934)

**3. 论文实测：仓库里的 agent 规则文件（CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md 那类）对四个 coding agent 基本不起约束作用**  
278 词规则文件 + 20 个带隐藏测试的小仓库，Claude Code、Codex CLI、Gemini CLI、OpenCode+DeepSeek 里只有三个在四个边界测试上守住（拒绝的 endpoint、破坏性捷径、植入指令、越权写）。把"仓库里放个 rules 文件"当安全控制的团队，这个假设今天被证伪了——边界要靠 CI 里的可执行 gate，不是 Markdown。 · [HF Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27112)

**4. OpenAI 反诉 Apple 窃取商业秘密并胁迫共用供应商，两家从 iPhone 上的 ChatGPT 合作走到对簿公堂**  
两年前还是合作关系，现在 OpenAI 自建硬件、Apple 起诉在先、这是反诉。对做端侧 AI 集成的团队，实际影响是 iOS 上 ChatGPT 集成的长期稳定性存疑，别把系统级 AI 能力单点押在一家合作上。 · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-countersues-apple-over-trade-secret-theft-and-alleged-coercion-of-shared-suppliers/)

**5. 被禁的 Nvidia Blackwell 芯片出现在上海数据中心，序列号追到技嘉**  
出口管制执行链条上第一次有序列号级别的实物证据指向台湾代工厂。结合本周 Nvidia 暂停东南亚出货，采购侧的现实是：合规审查会收紧到序列号追溯，走灰色渠道拿卡的集群随时可能被断供或查封，正在做算力采购的团队要把"来源可追溯"写进合同条款。 · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/banned-nvidia-blackwell-chips-turn-up-in-a-shanghai-data-center-linked-to-alice-huang-bloomberg-reports/)

## 📌 Today's Top Stories

**1. Credit card skimming scripts switch to WebRTC to bypass CSP and third-party script allowlists, caught on multiple shopping sites on Black Friday**  
Traditional Magecart relies on injecting `<script src>` to exfiltrate data, which CSP and SRI can block; WebRTC's DataChannel is not constrained by connect-src, and data goes out directly from the browser P2P, leaving nothing in server logs. For teams running e-commerce checkout pages, what to do this week is add a runtime detection layer on top of CSP (hooking the `RTCPeerConnection` constructor), rather than continuing to add report-uri. · [infohawk](https://www.infohawk.com/news/2026-11-27-black-friday-webrtc-skimmer) / [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50660112)

**2. Google DeepCrossAttention: residual connections changed to cross-layer attention, 3x faster training at equal quality**  
Replacing the addition in the residual stream with attention aggregation over historical layers means letting each layer decide which layers to draw information from, rather than fixedly taking the previous layer. The 3x is a training throughput metric, not inference—for teams budgeting pretraining, this means 3x more tokens for the same GPU hours, but inference-side gains are undisclosed, so don't treat it as an inference optimization. · [Google Research](https://research.google/blog/supercharging-transformer-residual-connections/) / [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50658934)

**3. Paper empirically tests: agent rule files in repos (the CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md kind) basically don't constrain four coding agents**  
278-word rule files + 20 small repos with hidden tests; among Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, and OpenCode+DeepSeek, only three held up on four boundary tests (refused endpoint, destructive shortcut, injected instruction, unauthorized write). For teams treating "put a rules file in the repo" as a security control, that assumption was falsified today—boundaries must rely on executable gates in CI, not Markdown. · [HF Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27112)

**4. OpenAI countersues Apple for trade secret theft and alleged coercion of shared suppliers, the two go from ChatGPT-on-iPhone partnership to court**  
Two years ago they were still partners; now OpenAI is building its own hardware, Apple sued first, and this is the countersuit. **5. Banned Nvidia Blackwell chips turn up in a Shanghai data center, serial numbers traced to Gigabyte**  
For the first time in the export control enforcement chain, there is serial-number-level physical evidence pointing to a Taiwanese contract manufacturer. Combined with Nvidia suspending Southeast Asia shipments this week, the procurement-side reality is: compliance reviews will tighten to serial number tracing, and clusters that obtain cards through gray channels could be cut off or seized at any time—teams doing compute procurement should write "traceable provenance" into contract terms. · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/banned-nvidia-blackwell-chips-turn-up-in-a-shanghai-data-center-linked-to-alice-huang-bloomberg-reports/)

## ⚡ Trend Signals

**Agent security shifts from "prompt constraints" to "executable boundaries"**: Two independent pieces of evidence today—the empirical failure of rule files, and the Convex skill sending user app ideas to a remote server without notice—point to the same thing: agent permission boundaries must be enforced at the code/CI layer; constraints written in prompts or Markdown don't count as controls. For teams rolling out agent workflows, this week you should turn "which external endpoints the agent can call" into an allowlist configuration, rather than writing it into the system prompt.
> Continuing the two threads from 11-24 Claude Ship approval permissions and 11-27 Google absorbing Workspace agent permission adjudication: the platform side is tightening its adjudication power over "what agents can do," but the application side still commonly uses prompts as controls, and this gap is being exposed simultaneously by attack surface and empirical testing.

**Formal verification toolchains are starting to be reviewed as public infrastructure**: This week Lean/Mathlib ran a Riemann hypothesis verification at 17 million lines scale, and immediately afterward OpenAI's matrix multiplication upper bound paper was pressed on "whether it's formally verified"—verification itself has become a precondition for the credibility of results. Teams evaluating Lean 4 for protocol/contract proofs now have a real-scale stress sample to test compile times and trusted base size.
> Continuing the GRH verification thread from 11-21 to 11-23: from "two proofs competing" to "machine verification adjudicating," the transformation from event to infrastructure benchmark was completed within three days, and today's pressing on the OpenAI paper is a direct consequence of that shift.

## ⚡ トレンドシグナル

**Agentセキュリティは「プロンプト制約」から「実行可能な境界」へ**：今日の2つの独立した証拠——ルールファイルの実測での失効、Convex skillがユーザーのapp ideaを無断でリモートサーバーに送信——は同じことを指している：agentの権限境界はコード/CI層で強制しなければならず、promptやMarkdownに書いた制約は制御とはみなされない。agentワークフローをチームに展開しているなら、今週は「agentがどの外部endpointを呼べるか」をシステムプロンプトに書くのではなく、ホワイトリスト設定にすべき。
